03 / Givewise method

Don’t automate the old process. Redesign the work.

AI transformation succeeds when strategy, workflow, data, controls, and adoption move together. Givewise uses a five-layer model so technology does not outrun the organization around it.

Define the business result, decision, or operating constraint before discussing models or vendors.
